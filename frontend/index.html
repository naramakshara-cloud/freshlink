Connecting Surplus Food to Those Who Need It Most

FreshLink matches edible surplus food from college hostels, campus canteens, households, and restaurants to nearby verified shelters, NGOs, and families before it spoils.

⏱️
Freshness Decay & Urgency
Food quality is modeled with half-life decay curves and hard safety bounds. Donors post once, and our algorithm prioritizes urgent pickups before expiration.
📍
Hyperlocal PostGIS Matching
Using spatial GIS geometry, food is routed to nearby verified receivers within their exact operational radius to minimize transport delays and transit spoilage.
🐾
Human & Animal Safety Priority
Human shelters receive top allocation priority. Animal shelters are matched exclusively with food explicitly verified as safe for animal consumption.
🔒
Confidential Handover Codes
Every listing generates a secret 4-digit pickup code known only to the donor. Receivers enter it on site to confirm physical handover and track delivery.
